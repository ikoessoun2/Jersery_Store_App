# GENERAL ARCHITECTURE
I would implement a model -> repository -> service -> view -> url architecture.
The apps are in 5 categories
1. The Catalog - The catalog is the storage of the various items we sell and the facilities for managing them.
2. The Inventory - The inventory is to record the amount of the various items in storage and provide facilities to manage the stock.
3. The records - The records are for reciepts of purchases and transactions. They would also store history.
4. The communication - The communication would manage the app communication to store and app to customer as well as notifications.
5. The payments - The payment application is to handle payments.
